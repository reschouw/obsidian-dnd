# Hook
The Party has been hired by the Director of the bardic college of Veridia to investigate why the Great Bell of Veridia has gone silent and stop panic spreading throughout the city. 

# Truths
*What separates this campaign from all others?*
*These will be shared with the players*
1. Veridia is a well-known place of pilgrimage for musicians of all kinds, and is home to one of the oldest and most renown bardic colleges: **The Lyceum of Resonant Harmony**
2. The Great Bell of Veridia, forged millennia ago by great dwarven artisans has recently gone silent, despite the best efforts of the college's bards.
3. Rumors are spreading rapidly of bards who have gone missing, spreading panic throughout the city.
4. While the great bell bell has been the talk of the town, rumors are spreading rapidly of other musicians or instruments becoming silent
5. Veridia has become rich because of the college, but it has also become completely reliant on it. Veridia's Governor is less than pleased about this situation.   

# Fronts
*Defined as a name, a goal, and three grim portents*
*Each portent below now folds in An-Yin's actual methodology (formerly the separate "Steps" section), so the in-fiction signs and his plan live in one place.*

An-Yin (Silence the City):
**Goal:** Achieve total silence so he can finally hear the "perfect note" in the space between sounds.

1. **The Beggar's Tune.** *(✅ Completed — Session 1.)* An-Yin arrived in Veridia disguised as a humble street musician, busking on corners and in market squares. His subtly corrupting tune went unnoticed by most, but it was potent enough to claim its first real victim: the Great Bell of Veridia went silent despite the bards' best efforts. When the party moved to point Director Seldon toward An-Yin, An-Yin killed him and turned on the party directly. They fought him off and he retreated — but not before leaving a silence curse on Anara, Toy Soldier, and Brad, who are now stuck hearing the horrifying sound of their own insides and flesh. Between Seldon's murder and an open battle with a hostile musician, the city has tipped into full-blown panic.
2. **The City's Chorus Fades.** *(⚠️ Active — triggered at the end of Session 1.)* With his cover already blown, An-Yin drops any remaining pretense. No more disguise, no more hiding in plain sight — he moves openly through the city, wantonly silencing anyone and anything he can get his pipa on. The once-background rumors of other instruments and musicians going silent are now confirmed and spreading fast. This is the city's chorus fading for real: an active, ongoing threat rather than a looming one.
3. **The Bell's Last Ring.** *(Not yet triggered.)* For his final act, An-Yin returns to the (already-silenced) Great Bell and corrupts it outright, using it to trigger a resonance cascade: the echoes of every masterpiece ever performed within Veridia are violently twisted and consumed as fuel, replaced by the echo of his own music. Once the cascade completes, the entire city is permanently silenced, and everyone inside is doomed to a slow decay.

**Ongoing complication:** Anara, Toy Soldier, and Brad are all carrying An-Yin's parting silence curse (hearing their own insides/flesh) — worth resolving, explaining, or escalating in Session 2 prep.

An-Yin: An ancient, immortal musician, seeking the perfect note. Driven mad by his pursuit, he seeks to silence all other sound in the world in order to pursue his perfect note.
