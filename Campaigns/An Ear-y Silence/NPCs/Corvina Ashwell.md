Once the most brilliant scholar of the Lyceum of Resonant Song and a master of sonic magic, Corvina Ashwell is now a tragic figure of the city's peril. 

In her desperation to figure out why pockets of silence were appearing in the city, she performed a ritual that backfired horribly, causing her to lose almost all of her hearing and nearly her sanity. She now must use a large ornate ear trumpet to head just about anything.

She attempted to dissect the magic causing the silence, but got trapped in it. Her quick thinking had her using her sonic magic to create a boom loud enough to break through the silence, although she nearly killed herself and lost her hearing in the process. 

She might have the most knowledge about what is going on in the city, but the party might have trouble getting it out of her.
