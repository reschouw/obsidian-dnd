**Status: Deceased** *(Session 1)*

Director Seldon is the stoic and proud head of the Lyceum of Resonant Song. While many of the professors (and certainly the students) at the college are exuberant and annoying, Director Seldon is serious, and carries authority that is obvious for everyone to see. A excellent harp player, and a learned historian as well, he seeks to guide those at the college to make good choices. Or at least slightly better ones...

With the growing threat of silence and the town's growing paranoia, he is stretched thin trying to manage the situation. 

## Death
During Session 1, Seldon hired the party to investigate the silencing of the Great Bell. While walking them through the college grounds, the party spotted a shadowy figure — An-Yin — playing pipa in a tree outside a student's window, and pointed him out to Seldon. An-Yin murdered him on the spot before turning on the party. His death, in full view of the party and a public street, is a major driver of the city's slide into full-blown panic (see [[Campaigns/An Ear-y Silence/0 - Overview|Overview]] — Front, Portent 1).

With Seldon gone, the party no longer has a direct point of contact at the college. [[Campaigns/An Ear-y Silence/NPCs/Corvina Ashwell|Corvina Ashwell]] is positioned to step into that role going forward.
