His name is Herb. He's a Herbalist. He sells HERBS. NOTHING ELSE.

A goblin escapee from the hovels beneath Grayspire — think Gollum from The Lord of the Rings: cagey, possessive, paranoid about anyone getting near his stash.

He has a hidden trapdoor leading to a basement completely filled with illicit drugs. Also booby traps.
