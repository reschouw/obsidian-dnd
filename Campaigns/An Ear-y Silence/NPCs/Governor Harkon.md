A shrewd, wrinkled old lizardfolk, wracked by nostalgia for the "old days", back when Veridia was the capital of one of the richest, most powerful nations in this part of the world. 

Over time it's splendor has faded, leaving Governor Harkon ruling an admittedly wealthy, but small city-state. It's politics are almost completely dominated by the [Lyceum of Resonant Harmony], the only remaining attraction to this little city state. What the college wants, it gets, and Harkon seeths over the lack of control and power.
