Like the musicians from Kung Fu Hustle. Long gross fingernails, casts ranged attacks by strumming his pipa

An-Yin is be a combination of Chinese characters meaning "peace" and "sound. 

An-Yin used to be a legendary Pipa player, but over years and years of performing for the rich and famous and powerful slowly went mad. He is now obsessed with the idea that the perfect chord isn't something that can be played, rather it's the silence between the notes. 

After being scoffed at by the [The Lyceum of Resonant Song], he now seeks to perfect his silence no matter the cost. 

His efforts aren't perfect at first, but the power chases seems to be coming within his grasp.

**Traits**

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** An-Yin makes two attacks with his Resonant Plectrum or casts one cantrip and makes one melee attack.
- **Resonant Plectrum.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage.
- **Dissonant Pluck (Cantrip).** _Ranged Spell Attack:_ +5 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 9 (2d8) thunder damage.
    
- **The Dreadful Pause (Recharge 5–6).** An-Yin strikes a silent strum across his instrument, targeting one creature he can see within 40 feet. The target must succeed on a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or fall under the maddening vacuum of absolute interior silence for 1 minute (concentration).
    
    - While affected, the creature is deafened to all outside sound, cannot speak, and cannot cast spells requiring a verbal component.
        
    - At the start of each of its turns, the target is overwhelmed by the grotesque, magnified sounds of its own anatomy—blood surging, muscle fibers sliding, and eyeballs grating in their sockets—taking **9 (2d8) psychic damage**.
        
    - The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
        

**Reactions**

- **Dead Air.** When a creature within 30 feet casts a spell with a verbal component, An-Yin creates an instantaneous dampening field. The caster must make a **DC 13 Constitution saving throw**. On a failure, the verbal component is choked out and the spell fails, wasting the action (but not the spell slot).
    

**Legendary Actions**

An-Yin can take **2 legendary actions**, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. An-Yin regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

- **Rest Measure.** An-Yin moves up to his speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
    
- **Strum of Discord (Costs 2 Actions).** An-Yin plucks a muted chord. All creatures within 10 feet must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**, taking 7 (2d6) force damage and being pushed 10 feet away on a failure, or half as much on a success.
