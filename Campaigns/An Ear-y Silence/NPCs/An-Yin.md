An-Yin is be a combination of Chinese characters meaning "peace" and "sound. 

An-Yin used to be a legendary Pipa player, but over years and years of performing for the rich and famous and powerful slowly went mad. He is now obsessed with the idea that the perfect chord isn't something that can be played, rather it's the silence between the notes. 

After being scoffed at by the [The Lyceum of Resonant Song], he now seeks to perfect his silence no matter the cost. 

His efforts aren't perfect at first, but the power chases seems to be coming within his grasp.
