Well funded, professional, and well-equipped, the City Watch of Veridia are a force to be reckoned with. Their allegiance is somewhat split between the college and the governor, although most lean towards the college. 

Their uniform is gleaming metal armor, with one shoulder pauldron embossed with the crest of the bardic college, and the other embossed with the crest of Veridia. They carry a variety of weapons, from longswords to crossbows, all of good quality and well maintained.
