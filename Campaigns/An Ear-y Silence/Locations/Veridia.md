Veridia is a sparkling city on the sea. It is known for being almost completely dominated by it's bardic college, the pompously named [Lyceum of Resonant Harmony]. 

While technically governed by a Govenor who resides in Castle Veridia, it's really the college that has the greatest power in Veridia. 

# The Lyceum of Resonant Harmony

The vast tower known, somewhat pompously as the Lyceum of Resonant Song sits in the bustling heart of Veridia.

Taller by far than Castle Veridia, it actually casts a shadow over the Governor's office, much to his distain. 

Topped with a giant majestic bell that should toll out the hour, it has recently failed to make a sound, despite the bard's best efforts. 

# The Boring Bard

The Boring Bard is the only establishment in all of Veridia that does NOT allow music. Located in the Northern Docks where the college staff live and congregate, it's always filled to the brim with happy patrons, glad to be away from the constant racket the college creates.
