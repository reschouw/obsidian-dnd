### Their Angle

2. Has information (true or false?)

## Magic Items: Quick Table
**When the party deserves loot but you haven't prepped:**

Roll 1d6 for tier, then pick something narrative:

1. **Common** (healing potion, rope of entanglement, cloak of billowing)
2. **Uncommon** (boots of speed, bag of tricks, ring of water walking)
3. **Rare** (cloak of invisibility, ring of protection, wand of fireball)
4. **Very Rare** (ring of three wishes, bag of holding, legendary weapon)

*Then: How does this connect to the story? Who made it? What's its history?*

## Use This Table When...
- A scene ends faster than expected
- The party splits up and you need parallel content
- You realize you forgot to prep an encounter
- A random encounter makes more narrative sense than your plan
- You need to roll for NPC reactions

**Remember:** You don't need these to be detailed. One sentence is enough. Your description and the party's reaction makes it real.
